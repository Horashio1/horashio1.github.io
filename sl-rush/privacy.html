SL Rush

Privacy Policy

App: SL Rush (Android, package com.colomborush.game)
Effective date: 27 October 2026

SL Rush is a driving game set on the roads of Sri Lanka. This policy explains what information the game and the advertising partner it uses handle, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not ask for, collect or store any personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to serve those ads.

1. Information we collect

None. SL Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no online leaderboards. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information, and we run no servers that receive data from the game.

Your progress and settings (selected vehicle, map, sound and camera options, best scores, coins and how many runs you have played) are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version of SL Rush shows full-screen ads between runs, served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show, measure and protect those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID, and app-set ID
  • IP address, which Google may use to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, taps on ads, and ad performance
  • Diagnostics about the ad SDK, such as crash logs and load times

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and may show personalised ads based on it where the law and your settings allow. The data is encrypted in transit. How Google handles it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. If you are in these regions, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Privacy option.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Play offline. The game works without an internet connection. With no connection, no ads are loaded and nothing is sent.
  • Delete everything. Uninstalling the game removes all data it stored on your device.

4. Permissions

SL Rush requests only the network permissions the ad SDK needs (internet access and network state) and the Advertising ID permission. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files or phone.

5. Children

SL Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. The only party that receives data from the game is Google, as described in section 2.

7. Security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, for example by adding online features or other partners, we will update this page and the effective date above before that version of the game is released.
